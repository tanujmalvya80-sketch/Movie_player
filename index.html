Frequent Asked Questions

How much does Netflix cost?

+

Netflix offers different plans with different features and pricing. You can choose a plan that works best for you and your viewing needs.

You can change your plan or cancel your subscription at any time. Prices and available plans may vary depending on your location.

Where can I watch Netflix?

+

You can watch Netflix on a wide range of internet-connected devices, including smart TVs, smartphones, tablets, laptops and streaming devices.

Simply sign in to your Netflix account on a supported device and start watching your favorite shows and movies.

How do I create a Netflix account?

+

Creating a Netflix account is simple. Visit the Netflix website or open the Netflix app and follow the instructions to sign up.

Choose a plan, enter your account details and complete the signup process to start watching Netflix.

Can I cancel Netflix anytime?

+

Yes, you can cancel your Netflix membership whenever you want. There is no long-term contract that requires you to stay subscribed.

After cancelling, you can generally continue watching until the end of your current billing period.

What can I watch on Netflix?

+

Netflix has a large collection of movies, TV shows, documentaries, anime and original productions from different countries and genres.

The available titles can change over time, so there is always something different to explore and discover.

Does Netflix work on multiple devices?

+

Yes, Netflix can be used on many supported devices, depending on your membership plan.

You can sign in to your account on compatible TVs, phones, tablets, computers and other supported streaming devices.
